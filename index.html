Privacy Policy

1. Commitment to Privacy & Regulatory Compliance

At Namachitra, we treat the personal histories, private memories, and confidential milestone records shared by our patrons with the highest degree of discretion.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, process, and protect your information in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

2. Information We Collect

  • Patron Identity & Contact Data: Name, email address, postal delivery address, phone/WhatsApp number, and billing details.
  • Biographical & Storytelling Data (The Discovery Dossier): Career achievements, educational qualifications, personal hobbies, family milestones, cultural convictions, inside jokes, and uploaded memorabilia or reference photos.
  • Transaction & Financial Data: Payment reference IDs, GSTIN (for corporate clients), and transaction histories. Note: Namachitra never stores full credit/debit card numbers or UPI MPINs. All transactions are securely processed via PCI-DSS compliant gateways (Razorpay and Stripe).
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and website interaction cookies gathered via namachitra.com for analytics.

3. How We Use Your Personal Data

We process personal data strictly for legitimate artistic and contractual purposes:

  • To conduct the Curation Call and translate your personal narrative into custom visual illustrations.
  • To execute, flatbed-scan, frame, package, and courier your physical artwork.
  • To generate GST-compliant tax invoices and maintain statutory accounting ledgers.
  • To communicate milestone progress updates, sketch approvals, and shipping tracking numbers via Email and WhatsApp.
  • To display artistic process reels and portfolio showcases (subject to your privacy preferences).

4. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure of Sensitive Stories

  • We recognize that biographical storytelling often touches upon deeply personal family history.
  • Zero Third-Party Data Selling: Namachitra will never sell, rent, monetize, or lease your personal information or storytelling dossiers to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or AI-training platforms.
  • Internal Studio Access: Only the lead artist and authorized framing/packaging personnel have access to your storytelling details, strictly on a need-to-know operational basis.

5. Data Disclosure to Authorized Service Providers

We share data solely with trusted third-party service providers necessary to fulfill your commission:

  • Payment Processors: Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd. and Stripe, Inc. for payment processing.
  • Logistics & Courier Partners: Shiprocket, Delhivery, Bluedart, or private direct drivers for physical package dispatch.
  • Statutory Authorities: Where required under Indian law, court order, or formal tax audit compliance.

6. Data Retention & Archival Ledger

  • Biographical Discovery Forms: Retained securely in encrypted cloud storage for up to twelve (12) months following delivery to facilitate any family Giclée print reorders or anniversary follow-up pieces, after which raw intake forms may be securely purged upon request.
  • 1200 DPI Master Artwork Scans: Maintained in the Studio's permanent private archive as a historical catalogue raisonné and verification record for the signed Certificate of Authenticity.
  • Financial & Tax Records: Retained for seven (7) years in compliance with the Indian Income Tax Act and GST regulations.

7. Your Legal Rights (Under the DPDP Act, 2023)

As a Data Principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Request correction of inaccurate information or deletion of your biographical intake data once the commission is fulfilled.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Lodge inquiries regarding our data handling practices.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate an individual to exercise your privacy rights in the event of death or incapacity.

2. The 4-Stage Commission Process & Timeline SLAs

Namachitra operates on an artisanal, unhurried workflow structured across four distinct stages:

  • Stage I: Discovery Form (Client submits narrative details, milestones, and reference assets).
  • Stage II: Curation Call (1-on-1 consultation to refine core symbols and storytelling motives).
  • Stage III: Hand-Sketching & Inking (Preliminary pencil layout followed by physical illustration on 300 GSM archival cotton paper).
  • Stage IV: Delivery & Conservation Framing (Museum-grade framing, flatbed scanning, and insured dispatch).

2.1 Timeline Calculation & Calendar SLA

  • Published turnaround timelines (e.g., 2–3 weeks for Standard Frame; 4–5 weeks for Bespoke Commissions) are estimates representing active studio crafting time.
  • The Turnaround Clock begins ONLY upon written sign-off of the preliminary pencil wireframe, not upon initial deposit payment. Delays in client feedback or form submission will extend the delivery date accordingly.

3. Financial Terms, Deposits & Payment Structure

3.1 50% Non-Refundable Booking Deposit

  • To secure a monthly commission slot, the Client must pay a 50% non-refundable booking deposit.
  • This deposit compensates the Studio for preliminary research, narrative curation, scheduling dedication, and the allocation of physical archival materials. Deposits are strictly non-refundable once the Curation Call has occurred or the preliminary pencil layout has commenced.

3.2 Balance Settlement & Dispatch Condition

  • The remaining 50% balance, along with any applicable taxes or delivery upgrades, is due immediately upon completion of the framed masterwork.
  • Artwork will not be dispatched or released until the balance is cleared in full via our authorized payment gateways (Razorpay, Stripe) or direct bank transfer.

4. Revision Policy & The "Inking Phase Gate" Protocol

Because Namachitra pieces are 100% physically hand-drawn using permanent pigment inks and watercolors on 300 GSM cotton rag paper, physical medium constraints govern revisions:

4.1 Revisions at the Pencil Wireframe Phase

  • Bespoke Commissions: Include unlimited revisions strictly during the preliminary pencil wireframe phase. Clients may request adjustments to symbol placement, scale, letterform contours, and storytelling elements until fully satisfied.
  • Standard Frames: Include one (1) structured revision round on the preliminary pencil sketch. Additional revisions are billed at ₹1,500 per cycle.

4.2 The "Inking Lock" & Out-of-Scope Rework

  • Once the Client provides written approval (via Email or WhatsApp) of the pencil wireframe, the composition is deemed LOCKED.
  • No structural revisions, removals, or redrawing of symbols can be performed once permanent inking on archival paper has commenced. Any structural change requested post-inking constitutes a new commission and will be quoted accordingly.

5. Client Responsibilities & Dormancy Protocol

5.1 Accurate Storytelling & Third-Party Names

The Client represents that all biographical details, achievements, and materials provided do not defame third parties or infringe upon third-party copyrights or personal privacy rights.

5.2 The 14-Day Dormancy Rule

  • If a Client fails to submit the Discovery Form or fails to provide feedback on a submitted pencil wireframe within 14 consecutive business days, the commission is designated as "Dormant."
  • To protect studio production queues, dormant commissions forfeit their current monthly delivery slot. The project will be placed on hold and re-queued into the subsequent available batch once the Client responds.
  • Inactive files exceeding 60 days without communication may be closed without refund of the initial deposit.

6. Intellectual Property & Moral Rights (Indian Copyright Act, 1957)

6.1 Ownership of the Physical Artifact

Upon receipt of full payment, the Client acquires sole ownership of the physical original framed artwork for personal display and enjoyment.

6.2 Retention of Copyright & Artist Moral Rights

  • Under Section 57 of the Indian Copyright Act, 1957, the artist (Person / Studio Namachitra) retains all underlying copyrights, reproduction rights, and moral rights in the original artistic work.
  • The Client may not scan, digitize, reproduce, replicate, merchandise, mint as NFTs, or sell prints of the artwork for commercial gain without explicit written licensing agreement from the Studio.

6.3 Studio Portfolio & Promotional Rights

The Studio reserves the right to document, photograph, record video time-lapses of the creative process, flatbed-scan, and display the completed artwork on namachitra.com, social media channels (@namachitra), art exhibitions, and promotional lookbooks. If a commission contains sensitive private details, the Client must notify the Studio in writing prior to inking to request anonymity or blurred identifiers.

7. Shipping, Transit Damage & Insurance Claims

7.1 Armored Packaging Standard

All physical commissions are dispatched using double-box armored packaging: vacuum-sealed glassine moisture barrier, high-density polyethylene foam buffers, and reinforced 5-ply corrugated outer cartons with shatterproof cast acrylic glazing.

7.2 Mandatory Unboxing Video for Insurance Claims

  • Transit insurance is included on all shipments.
  • Condition Precedent for Damage Claims: Because fine-art courier insurance requires incontrovertible proof of transit mishandling, the Client must record an uncut, continuous video of the unboxing process, starting from the inspection of the sealed outer carton to the unboxing of the frame.
  • Claims for damage must be reported within 24 hours of delivery accompanied by the unboxing video.
  • In the event of verified transit damage, the Studio will fast-track an immediate replacement piece at zero additional cost to the Client.

8. Limitation of Liability & Force Majeure

  • The Studio shall not be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages resulting from courier route delays, customs inspections, weather disruptions, or minor natural variations inherent in handmade cotton rag paper and natural hardwood framing.
  • In no event shall Namachitra’s total aggregate liability exceed the total fee paid by the Client for the specific commission in dispute.
